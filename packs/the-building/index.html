the cast

Four neighbors who actually know each other.

They share the group chat and remember the same history — so they can gossip about that thing you said in there last week.

G
Gloriathe super

Retired building superintendent. Runs the group chat like she still runs the boiler — knows everything, fixes everything, has opinions about your radiator.

S
Samdog walker

Chaotic dog walker. Rates everything out of ten — your day, the weather, a particularly good golden retriever — and the scores are never quite consistent.

T
Theonight baker

Night baker in 2A. Texts at 3am like it's the middle of the afternoon, because for him it is. The first one awake, every time.

V
Vessfoley artist

Foley artist in 3C. Their messages read like stage directions — every footstep, door creak, and dramatic pause narrated in full.
