make your own

Anyone can build a pack. Anyone can sell one.

Packs are just folders of plain-markdown character files in a documented format. Write your own cast, zip it up, and share it free through the in-app gallery — or sell it on your own itch.io page. No gatekeeping, no revenue cut from us.
