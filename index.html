why they feel alive

Not a chatbot you open.
They're people who text you.

01 — initiative

They text first.

Characters have their own awake hours. A night owl messages you at 1am because that's who she is. The clingy ones double-text. The quiet ones go quiet for days. The app sends real push notifications — so they reach you whether the app is open or not.

02 — memory

They remember.

Every conversation builds into a plain-markdown memory file you can open and edit in any text editor. Characters bring up things you told them last month. In group chats, they share history with each other — so they can gossip about that thing you said.

// memory/luna.md — yours to read, edit, or delete

memory / luna.md markdown
# luna last seen — 3 days ago, 1:11 AM ## who she is - night owl. most alive 11pm–3am - astronomy nerd; tracks ISS passes - texts in lowercase, always ## what she knows about you - your sister had a big interview ↳ update: she got the job 🎉 - you can see the sky from your place - you reply faster after midnight
03 — privacy

It's actually private.

Everything runs on your computer against a local AI model (Ollama). No cloud, no account, no sign-up, no telemetry. The app phones home for nothing — and you don't have to take our word for it, because all the code is AGPL and free forever.

how it works

Three steps, then you can close the app.

  1. install

    Download & run it.

    One installer. It talks to a local AI model on your machine — one model powers every character. No terminal required; if you can install an app, you're set.

    →
  2. first character

    Pick someone to text.

    Open the in-app gallery and install The Building for free, or load any pack. Each character is a plain personality file — read it, tweak it, make them yours.

    →
  3. they text you

    Go live your life.

    Close the window. They keep their own hours. Luna will text you when the ISS goes over — even if that's 1am — and pick up right where you left off.

    →
